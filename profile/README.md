# UGtastic

**The 15-Year Midwest Software Craftsmanship Oral History Archive & Technical Dialogue Corpus (2009–2026).**

UGtastic captured the informal conversations, hallway dialogues, and pivotal transitions of software craftsmanship, early Ruby and Rails adoption, functional programming, and distributed systems movements across Chicago, the Midwest, and global developer conferences.

---

## 🎙️ The Archive at a Glance

- 📜 **100% Transcribed Dialogue:** 207 video interviews (~456,000 words) across Software Craftsmanship North America (SCNA), RailsConf, GOTO Chicago, ChicagoWebConf, and user group tables.
- 🕸️ [**Charlotte's Web of Wisdom & Tributes**](https://www.just3ws.com/web/) — 184 glowing speaker tributes & craftsmanship principles.
- 💎 [**Archive Curiosities & Prophecies**](https://www.just3ws.com/curiosities/) — Tech predictions vs. modern reality & lost tooling history.
- 📊 [**Corpus Pattern Intelligence**](https://www.just3ws.com/intelligence/) — Topic distributions, trope heatmaps, and sentiment analysis.
- 🗺️ [**Interactive Timeline**](https://www.just3ws.com/timeline/) & [**Speaker Directory**](https://www.just3ws.com/speakers/)
